1. **MAJOR — The selection-split confidence interval assumes independent tasks that share worktrees.** [eval_pack.py:80](/tmp/oxide-julia-1abb3d7/docs/julia-evidence-judge-eval/scripts/eval_pack.py:80) resamples individual tasks. In the saved data, the 70 `dev` tasks use only five parent worktrees. Resampling by worktree instead gives an R1 J1 selection-split coverage interval of **[−0.1266, +0.0156]**, versus the reported task-level **[−0.1382, −0.0163]**. The claim that this selection-split loss is statistically significant is therefore sensitive to the unit of resampling. The R1 J2 confirmation loss remains below zero under the same worktree-level check: **[−0.2239, −0.0649]**. **Disposition: unchanged.**

2. **MAJOR — The report over-attributes J2’s signal to presentation position.** [README.md:127](/tmp/oxide-julia-1abb3d7/docs/julia-evidence-judge-eval/README.md:127) and [README.md:225](/tmp/oxide-julia-1abb3d7/docs/julia-evidence-judge-eval/README.md:225) say its above-chance signal comes only from an order artifact. The first option does receive a large probability, but recomputing hash-order AUROC *without first-presented candidates* gives **0.612 on S** and **0.555 on C**, compared with 0.598 and 0.539 using all candidates. The order controls establish a strong position effect; they do not isolate all of J2’s signal as that effect. Its final-pack result still fails. **Disposition: unchanged.**

3. **MINOR — Four CB gold references do not match the saved parent worktrees.** The labels and coverage denominators come from gold line numbers ([eval_cand.py:36](/tmp/oxide-julia-1abb3d7/docs/julia-evidence-judge-eval/scripts/eval_cand.py:36), [eval_pack.py:57](/tmp/oxide-julia-1abb3d7/docs/julia-evidence-judge-eval/scripts/eval_pack.py:57)). A read-only path and line-range check found **three missing gold files** and **`package.json:109` in a 108-line file**. The scorer can also assign zero relevant-token credit when a gold file cannot be read ([eval_pack.py:24](/tmp/oxide-julia-1abb3d7/docs/julia-evidence-judge-eval/scripts/eval_pack.py:24)). Excluding those four tasks from the saved R1 J1 S metrics changes mean Δ coverage only from **−0.0772 to −0.0770**; relative efficiency changes from **−22.7% to −24.2%**. The labels need qualification, but this does not reverse the aggregate. **Disposition: unchanged.**

[README.md:205](/tmp/oxide-julia-1abb3d7/docs/julia-evidence-judge-eval/README.md:205) also says every formulation loses coverage significantly, while its own R1 J2 S interval crosses zero ([README.md:143](/tmp/oxide-julia-1abb3d7/docs/julia-evidence-judge-eval/README.md:143)). The measured J2 R2 grid fails, and the confirmation and runtime gates still support REJECT. **Disposition: unchanged.**

5. **MINOR — Memory and load-time wording mixes process totals with added cost.** The roughly **1.5 GB** real-pool figure is *peak process RSS*, not measured “extra RSS” as [README.md:64](/tmp/oxide-julia-1abb3d7/docs/julia-evidence-judge-eval/README.md:64) calls it. The six-thread phase-0 artifact records **225 MB before model load**, **832 MB after load**, and **4.21 s model load** plus **0.67 s import** ([t6-pcore.json:12](/tmp/oxide-julia-1abb3d7/docs/julia-evidence-judge-eval/results/t6-pcore.json:12)). Warm latency excludes both. The reported warm J2 median, **922 ms**, still exceeds the 250 ms gate. **Disposition: unchanged.**

6. **NOTE — Reproduction depends on machine-local inputs.** [README.md:246](/tmp/oxide-julia-1abb3d7/docs/julia-evidence-judge-eval/README.md:246) correctly says tasks, gold, dumps, scores, and corpora are local. The [checksum manifest](/tmp/oxide-julia-1abb3d7/docs/julia-evidence-judge-eval/results/SHA256SUMS) verifies 13 code/model artifacts, but does not pin those raw inputs. I verified all 13 listed hashes locally and found complete, unique task/dump/J1-score IDs for all six sets; another checkout cannot reproduce the published numbers from the study directory alone. **Disposition: unchanged; portability of reproduction is limited.**

**Direct checks:** `eval_pack.py cb,heldout,dev R1J1` reproduced **n=154, Δ coverage −0.0772 [−0.1382, −0.0163], relative efficiency −22.7%**. `eval_pack.py bcd,ca,c5 R1J2` reproduced **n=117, Δ coverage −0.1623 [−0.2450, −0.0820], relative efficiency −49.0%**. `eval_cand.py cb,heldout,dev J1` reproduced **2,131 candidates, AUROC 0.4932**. For CB task `049a7048`, sorting the saved baseline pool by role and its saved J1 scores exactly matched the R1 dump’s pool order.

The skipped fresh test limits any claim about fresh-data quality. It does **not** overturn REJECT under the stated synchronous runtime gate: the measured best real-pool J2 median is 922 ms against 250 ms. The task IDs do not overlap across S and C, and I found no scored candidate missing from its baseline pool; the confirmed problems above concern interpretation and reproducibility rather than an observed Julia-only gold or OXIDE-score leak.
